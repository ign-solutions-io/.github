## What is IGN Solutions?

IGN Solutions is an engineer-led custom software studio building fast, modern websites, business automations, and software integrations with **Next.js, React, and TypeScript**. We work directly with founders, operators, and technical teams who have outgrown templates and "good enough" solutions — no drag-and-drop builders, no offshore handoffs, no disappearing after launch.

The engineer who scopes your project writes the code, ships it in weeks not quarters, and is still around when you need the next thing. Every project is built to rank on Google, load in under a second, and stay maintainable five years from now.

## What we build

Four offerings, all built one way — engineer-led, owned end-to-end:

- **Custom Web Development** — No templates. No drag-and-drop builders. Just a fast, modern site built around your business. Loads instantly, ranks on Google, turns visitors into customers.
- **eCommerce Development** — Stores that don't break on Black Friday. Secure, mobile-first, and built so you can manage products without filing a support ticket.
- **Business Automation & Integrations** — Show us your 60-minute manual headaches and we'll turn them into 20-minute automated wins. If it's repetitive, it's a candidate.
- **Custom Web Apps** — Dashboards, internal tools, and full platforms built around how you actually work, not how some SaaS thinks you should.

## How we work

- **No templates, no builders.** Every site is custom Next.js + React + TypeScript. Templates look fast at the demo and slow at scale; builders rank poorly and lock you into a vendor that owns your data.
- **No subcontractors.** The engineer on the scoping call is the engineer writing the code, deploying it, and picking up the phone a year later.
- **Fixed, itemized quotes** after a 30-minute scoping call. No hourly billing, no surprise change orders. If we're not the right fit, we say so.
- **Weeks, not quarters.** Marketing sites ship in 3–6 weeks. Automations land in 2–4 weeks. Custom web apps depend on scope, but they ship in weeks.
- **Performance and SEO are non-negotiable.** Performance budgets enforced in CI. Server-rendered metadata. Structured data that matches the DOM. Core Web Vitals measured at the 75th percentile of real users — not on a developer's MacBook.
- **Post-launch ownership.** We deploy to your AWS account or ours — your call — and offer monthly retainers for security patches, dependency upgrades, and small feature work.

## Our stack

- **Web:** Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4
- **Infrastructure:** AWS via [SST](https://sst.dev). CloudFront, Lambda, S3 — the same primitives running TikTok, Notion, and Hulu at scale
- **Automation & backends:** Python and TypeScript — whichever earns its keep for the problem
- **Quality gates:** TypeScript strict, Vitest, ESLint, Prettier, performance budgets in CI, human PR review before every deploy

We pick boring, battle-tested tools that will still be supported in five years — not the framework-of-the-month.
